Leadership Styles in Multicultural Groups, Aritz and Walker 2014 study

Research Focus

Investigated how culture influences leadership behaviors and the resulting effects on participation, inclusion,
and satisfaction in multicultural teams, specifically between Americans and East Asians.




Key Leadership Styles Identified
• Directive Leadership
  • Characterized by minimal listening, abrupt topic shifts, and low
    elaboration.
  • Typically dominates the discourse, with one-way instruction flow.    • Collaborative Leadership
                                                                           • Encourages active participation, extended elaboration, and
• Cooperative Leadership                                                     mutual idea development between leader and team members
  • Involves moderate engagement from team members.
  • Some listening occurs, though still predominantly leader-led in
    shaping discussion.




22/06/2026      Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026                                                                     13
Leadership Styles in Multicultural Groups, Aritz and Walker 2014 study,
cont.


Outcomes & Insights
• Participation and Inclusion
  • East Asian participants often reported lower inclusion and satisfaction when leadership was directive, partly due to
    disparities in English language proficiency and less opportunity to contribute.


• Importance of Communication Strategy
  • Using “redundant communication strategies”—like repeating key information across channels or rephrasing—helped
    improve understanding, especially for team members with limited language proficiency.


• Media Richness & Inclusion
  • Richer communication media (like video calls) enhanced feelings of inclusion and satisfaction, whereas leaner, text-centric
    media (e.g., email or forums) supported task accomplishment but may undermine relational aspects.

Cultural Intelligence and Leadership Styles, Saini & Banerjee 2024 study

Research Focus:

Explores how cultural intelligence (CQ) and leadership styles interact to influence productivity
and satisfaction in multicultural teams.

                                                        Cultural Intelligence is abbreviated as CQ by analogy with IQ (Intelligence
                                                        Quotient) and EQ (Emotional Intelligence / Emotional Quotient).


                                                        The “Q” stands for Quotient (a standardized measure or capability).
                                                        CQ therefore signals a measurable capability related to how effectively a person
                                                        can function across cultures.


                                                        So just like IQ = Cognitive ability and EQ = Emotional ability,
                                                        CQ = Cultural capability/intelligence.

Cultural Intelligence and Leadership Styles, Saini & Banerjee 2024 study

Research Focus:

Explores how cultural intelligence (CQ) and leadership styles interact to influence productivity
and satisfaction in multicultural teams.



Key Leadership Style Identified


• Transformational Leadership: Emphasizes inspiring vision, individualized
  consideration, and enabling followers to transcend routine.


Combined with high Cultural Intelligence (CQ), transformational leadership
becomes a powerful for enhancing outcomes in multicultural settings.

Cultural Intelligence and Leadership Styles, Saini & Banerjee 2024 study,

Main Findings
• Leaders with high CQ who use transformational leadership drive:
  ❑ Improved team collaboration
  ❑ Stronger team cohesion
  ❑ Increased organizational productivity


• Multicultural teams led by such leaders report higher job satisfaction, thanks to:
  ❑ Inclusive leadership climates
  ❑ Supportive cross-cultural environments

Conflict Management in Diverse Teams


Evidence-based tips


1. Recognize Cultural Conflict Triggers


  ❑ Different communication norms (direct vs. indirect)
  ❑ Power distance expectations (hierarchical vs. egalitarian)
  ❑ Varied attitudes toward conflict (avoidance vs. confrontation)




        Tip: Train leaders to decode cultural signals before
                         escalating issues.




22/06/2026   Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026         43
Conflict Management in Diverse Teams , cont.


Evidence-based tips


2. Use Collaborative Problem-Solving


  ❑ Apply collaborative leadership → encourage shared discussion
    and idea elaboration (Aritz & Walker, 2014).
  ❑ Avoid overly directive styles in diverse teams, which reduce
    inclusion.



      Tip: Facilitate structured turn-taking so all voices are
                               heard.




22/06/2026   Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026       44
Conflict Management in Diverse Teams , cont.


Evidence-based tips


3. Build Psychological Safety


  ❑ Create a climate where mistakes, dissent, and diverse views are
    safe to express.
  ❑ Inclusive leadership behaviours (listening, valuing uniqueness) are
    strongly linked to lower turnover (ρ=–.52) and higher creativity
    (ρ≈.44).

      Tip: Leaders should explicitly invite input from quieter
                         cultural groups.




22/06/2026   Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026              45
Conflict Management in Diverse Teams , cont.


Evidence-based tips


4. Adapt Conflict Styles with CQ
Leaders with high Cultural Intelligence (CQ) can flex between:


  ❑ Integrating (collaborative, win-win)
  ❑ Compromising (balanced)
  ❑ Accommodating (relationship-preserving, useful in high-context
    cultures)


     Tip: Teach leaders to diagnose when to adapt vs. when to
                    push for direct resolution.




22/06/2026   Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026         46
Conflict Management in Diverse Teams , cont.


Evidence-based tips


5. Leverage Mediation & Neutral Spaces


  ❑     Use third-party facilitators when conflicts are culturally charged.
  ❑     Neutral ground rules (e.g., structured agendas, shared decision-
        making) prevent dominance by one cultural style.




             Tip: Rotate facilitators across cultural subgroups.




22/06/2026       Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026              47
Conflict Management in Diverse Teams, cont.


Evidence-based tips


6. Focus on Common Goals


  ❑ Frame discussions around shared outcomes (project success,
    innovation).
  ❑ Research shows diversity’s positive effect on performance is
    strongest in complex tasks—
  leaders can harness this by keeping focus on collective problem-
  solving.

      Tip: Re-center debates on what unites the team, not what
                           divides them.
