  Team Roles (Belbin Roles)



Teams work better when people take different roles


A team is not just made of individuals, it is a
combination of different roles that contribute to
success.


Examples
 • Coordinator → organizes
 • Implementer → executes
 • Plant → creative thinker
 • Teamworker → supports others

Team Roles (Belbin Roles), cont.



Belbin identified that:
• People tend to behave in specific role
  patterns in teams


• Effective teams require a balance of
  different roles


• No single person can cover all roles
  effectively

Belbin Roles: Categories of Roles

1. Thinking Roles (Idea-oriented)

Focus: Ideas, analysis, problem solving


• Plant
  → Creative, generates innovative ideas
  Risk: May ignore details


• Monitor Evaluator
  → Logical, analytical, evaluates options
  Risk: May appear critical or slow


• Specialist
  → Deep expert knowledge in a specific area
  Risk: May focus too narrowly
                                                                  https://www.sabrehq.com/post/the-belbin-team-role-model-the-key-to-genuine-team-building




 08/06/2026     Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026                                                                                   21
Belbin Roles: Categories of Roles


2. Action Roles (Task-oriented)

Focus: Execution, results, getting things done


• Implementer
  → Turns ideas into practical actions
  Risk: Can resist new ideas


• Shaper
  → Drives the team forward, challenges others
  Risk: Can be aggressive or confrontational


• Completer Finisher
  → Ensures quality, checks details, meets deadlines
  Risk: Can be perfectionist / overfocused on errors
                                                                 https://www.sabrehq.com/post/the-belbin-team-role-model-the-key-to-genuine-team-building




 08/06/2026    Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026                                                                                   22
Belbin Roles: Categories of Roles


3. Social Roles (People-oriented)

Focus: Relationships, teamwork, coordination


• Coordinator
  → Organises, delegates, keeps overview
  Risk: May delegate too much


• Teamworker
  → Supports others, builds harmony
  Risk: Avoids conflict


• Resource Investigator
  → Explores opportunities, networks externally
  Risk: May lose interest quickly                                https://www.sabrehq.com/post/the-belbin-team-role-model-the-key-to-genuine-team-building

Learned lessons



There is NO “best” role or team composition


• Too many idea roles → no execution
• Too many action roles → low creativity
• Too many people roles → slow decisions




 Effectiveness depends on:
• context (startup vs organisation)
• goals (innovation vs efficiency)
