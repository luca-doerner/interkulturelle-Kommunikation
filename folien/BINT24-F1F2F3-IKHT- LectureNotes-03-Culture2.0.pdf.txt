Behaviour: How human nature, culture, and personality interact

                         Human Nature
                         • Universal (all human) and inherited
                         • Basic instincts and needs shared by all humans
                         • Examples: hunger, fear of failure, desire to belong,
                           anger, etc

Culture
• Learned and group-specific
• Influences norms, values, behaviours, language
• Examples: Time orientation, Communication styles (direct vs.
  indirect), Attitudes toward hierarchy, etc…



                                                   Personality
                                                   • Individual differences
                                                   • Formed through a mix of inherited traits and learned experiences
                                                   • Explains variation within the same culture
                                                   • Example: Introvert / extrovert

Building Cultural Sensitivity: Key Approaches



1. Cultural Awareness & Knowledge:
 • Focus: Understanding specific cultural norms, values, communication styles (e.g., Hofstede's
   Dimensions).
 • How: Cultural training, information sharing, individual research.




2. Empathy & Perspective-Taking:
 • Focus: Ability to understand and share others' feelings from different cultural backgrounds.
 • How: Active listening, asking clarifying questions, promoting open dialogue.




 08/06/2026   Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026                                     33
Building Cultural Sensitivity: Key Approaches, cont.



3. Cultural Intelligence (CQ):
 • Focus: Capability to adapt effectively to new cultural contexts. Covers Cognitive (knowledge),
   Motivational (drive), Behavioural (action), and Metacognitive (strategy) aspects.
 • How: Self-assessment, targeted CQ training, diverse experiences.




4. Inclusive Leadership & Team Norms:
 • Focus: Leaders and team actively shaping an inclusive environment where diversity is leveraged.
 • How: Setting clear expectations, fostering psychological safety, celebrating diversity, addressing
   biases.




 08/06/2026   Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026                                           34
Building Cultural Sensitivity: Key Approaches




5. Adaptability & Flexibility (Very Important):
 • Focus: Willingness and ability to adjust behaviours, expectations, and processes to accommodate
   differences.
 • How: Flexible work arrangements, adapting communication, openness to new approaches.

  • Organizational Culture: The "personality" of a company – its shared values,
    beliefs, and practices.



  • Impact: Significantly influences how cultural sensitivity is perceived, developed,
    and practiced internally and externally.



  • Key Idea: Different organizational types foster or hinder cultural sensitivity in

Common Organizational Culture Types & Their Impact
Competing Values Framework (CVF)


 1. Clan Culture (Collaborate / Family):
   • Characteristics: People-oriented, friendly, collaborative,
     strong teamwork.
   • Impact on Cultural Sensitivity: High Potential. Naturally
     fosters empathy and open communication.
   • Challenge: Can become isolated if not proactively inclusive of
     all backgrounds.


 2. Adhocracy Culture (Create / Dynamic):
   • Characteristics: Innovative, flexible, risk-taking, focused on
     creativity.
   • Impact on Cultural Sensitivity: Mixed Potential. Values          https://www.shiftbase.com/glossary/company-culture-types
     diverse ideas.
   • Challenge: Fast pace can sometimes overshadow deliberate
     cultural understanding; implicit pressure to "fit in" quickly.


   08/06/2026     Prof. Lamya Abdullah - IKHT - BINT24 - SoSe2026                                                                51
Common Organizational Culture Types & Their Impact, cont



 3. Market Culture (Compete / Results-Oriented):
  • Characteristics: Results-driven, competitive, focused on
    profitability and goals.
  • Impact on Cultural Sensitivity: Lower Natural Tendency. Less
    inherent focus beyond direct business impact.
  • Challenge: "Sink or swim" mentality; cultural differences may be
    seen as obstacles to efficiency unless strategically valued.




 4. Hierarchy Culture (Control / Structured):
  • Characteristics: Process-oriented, stable, controlled, clear
    roles/rules, efficiency-focused.
  • Impact on Cultural Sensitivity: Formal but Potentially Rigid.       https://www.shiftbase.com/glossary/company-culture-types

  • Challenge: Rigidity can stifle individual expression; bureaucracy
