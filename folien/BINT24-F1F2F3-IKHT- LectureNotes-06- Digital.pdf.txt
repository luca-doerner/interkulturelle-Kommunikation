Best Practices for Global Teams


                                                               Effective Teams
Best Practices
• Use explicit language.
                                                               • Define roles clearly
• Avoid slang.
                                                               • Use shared documentation
• Confirm understanding.
                                                               • Agree on communication rules
• Summarize decisions.
                                                               • Schedule regular check-ins
• Use cameras strategically
                                                               • Make decisions transparent
• Document agreements.
                                                               • Consider (respect) time zones
                                                               • Rotate meeting times.
                                                               • Encourage inclusion

Technology does not eliminate cultural differences → it changes how they are expressed.


Successful digital collaboration requires:
• Clear communication
• Cultural intelligence
• Trust
• Inclusive leadership
• Effective digital tools
• Responsible AI use
