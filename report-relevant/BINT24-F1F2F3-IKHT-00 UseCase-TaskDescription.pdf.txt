                                        Use Case Report:
______________ Analysis of an Intercultural and Diverse Team _________________


                                            Task detail description
Objective
Select a real or fictional intercultural and heterogeneous team and conduct a structured
analysis using the models, theories, and frameworks discussed during the course.
The goal is to evaluate how team composition, communication patterns, leadership
approaches, and cultural diversity influence team performance and collaboration.

The detailed points are for completeness → you may pick what is suitable for your case.
-----------------------------------------

Step 1: Define the Team
Provide a detailed description of the team you have selected.
Team Composition
   • Who are the members of the team?
   • What are their professional backgrounds, expertise, and responsibilities?
   • What cultural, educational, personalities, or demographic (etc.) diversity exists within
        the team?
Team Context
   • Where are the team members located?
   • Is the team co-located, hybrid, or fully remote?
   • How do geographical distance and time zones affect collaboration?
Team Purpose
   • What is the team's primary objective?
   • What project, product, service, or business goal are they working toward?
   • What are the key performance expectations and deliverables?

-----------------------------------------

Step 2: Analyse the Team Dynamics
Apply relevant organizational behaviour, intercultural management, and team-development
frameworks to examine the following dimensions.
1. Team Formation and Development
    • How was the team formed?
    • Analyse the team's stage of development using models such as Tuckman's Forming–
        Storming–Norming–Performing–Adjourning model.
    • What factors contribute to the team's effectiveness or challenges during each stage?
2. Team Roles and Responsibilities
    • What formal and informal roles exist within the team?
    • Are responsibilities clearly defined?
    • Apply relevant theories such as Belbin's Team Roles to assess role diversity and team
        balance.

3. Communication Styles and Cultural Differences
    • How do team members communicate and exchange information?
    • What cultural dimensions influence communication behaviours?
    • Analyse potential differences using frameworks such as Hofstede's Cultural
        Dimensions, Hall's High- and Low-Context Communication, or other relevant
        intercultural models.
    • What impact do personality traits and communication preferences have on team
        interactions?
4. Leadership Style
    • What leadership approach is demonstrated within the team?
    • Is leadership centralized or distributed?
    • Evaluate the effectiveness of the leadership style using relevant leadership theories
        (e.g., Transformational, Directive, Cooperative, etc.. Leadership).
5. Conflict Analysis and Conflict Management
    • What types of conflict may arise (task, relationship, process, or cultural conflicts)?
    • Styles: Avoiding, compromising, etc...
    • What misunderstandings can occur because of cultural or communication differences?
    • How are conflicts addressed and resolved?
    • How are authority, decision-making, and accountability managed?
6. Collaboration Methods and Digital Tools (optional)
    • Which collaboration tools, technologies, and communication platforms are used?
    • How do these tools support knowledge sharing, coordination, and team cohesion?
    • What processes or practices help bridge cultural, geographical, or functional gaps?

-----------------------------------------
Step 3: Critical Evaluation and Recommendations
Based on your analysis:
     • Identify the team's key strengths.
     • Highlight major challenges and risks.
     • Evaluate how diversity contributes to innovation and performance.
     • Provide evidence-based recommendations for improving communication, leadership,
          collaboration, and overall team effectiveness.




Expected Outcome
The report should present a comprehensive, theory-based analysis that demonstrates the
ability to connect practical team dynamics with established organizational behaviour and
intercultural management frameworks. Conclusions and recommendations should be
supported by relevant models, concepts, and course materials.
